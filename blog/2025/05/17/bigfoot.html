この動画はYouTubeで数百万回の再生回数を記録し、ビッグフットの証拠として最も視聴された動画の一つとなりました。 カード氏と同行者は、ユタ州プロボ・キャニオンのスクワウ・ピークとリトル・ロック・キャニオン・オーバールック付近でキャンプをしていました。ハイキング中、彼らは最初に鹿の群れを目撃しました。その群れを追跡していたところ、クマだと考えた生物を発見しました。しかし、それが立ち上がり、彼らの方を直接見据えた瞬間、巨大な動物であることに気づきました。 （0:32:01）

グループはすぐに車に向かって走り、テントとほとんどの荷物を置き去りにしました。多くの研究者がこの動画の信憑性を支持しています。彼らは、目撃者の反応が信憑性がある理由として、過度に反応したり結論を急いだりしなかった点を指摘しています。また、彼らは最初クマだと考えていたが、それが立ち上がった瞬間に気づいたと述べています。

Moving ahead again to October 30th, 2012, a YouTube user named Beard Card, who people referred to as Mr. Card, uploaded a 48-second video. The video shows the filmmaker and several companions standing among autumn leaves. The camera pans up to reveal something on a hillside. A large, dark figure that appears to be crouching or rummaging on all fours. It suddenly stands upright, startling the witnesses, and everyone immediately flees the area. (0:31:33)

This video eventually accumulated millions of views on YouTube and became one of the most-watched bits of Bigfoot evidence ever recorded. Mr. Card and his companions were camping in Provo Canyon, Utah, near Squaw Peak and Little Rock Canyon Overlook. During their hike, they initially spotted a herd of deer. While following them, they noticed what they thought was a bear. But when it stood and looked directly at them, they realized it was a massive animal. (0:32:01)

The group immediately ran straight to the car, abandoning their tent and most of their possessions. Numerous researchers have supported the video's authenticity. They note that the witnesses' reactions are credible because they neither overreacted nor jumped to conclusions. And they initially thought that it was a bear until it stood upright.
