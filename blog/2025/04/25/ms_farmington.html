Key Points

  1. Military officer had the correct security clearance which gave him access to a classified materials library.

  2. Report seen by witness made reference to "crashes", and that bodies were recovered.

  3. In addition, diamond tipped drill bits, and acetylene torches were used to gain access to the interior (Reference 1946 craft seen at Wright Patterson AFB, and 40 foot diameter craft guarded by a U.S. Marine during in December of 1963)

  4. Eventually, technicians were able to gain access to the interior of the craft by way of a small entry hatch. The report said (referring to the door) that it was "almost as if the material of the craft liquified, and then solidified again"
