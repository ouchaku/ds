OCR

Location: Gondomar, Portugal

Date: middle of September 1977 Time: 1:30 a.m.

The witness, 38-year-old Jose Luis Morais Luna Almeida, a local banker, was watering his garden late at night when he noticed a strange figure that was about thirty meters away and looked like a very intense light source composed of dull gray-blue color which illuminated the background. The strange figure had a broad upper part and a head with the same dull gray-blue tone, having, however, in this same upper part a whitish-colored central square that had at its center a flashing orange light that flashed in and out with "extraordinary slowness." The witness calculated the dimensions of the strange humanoid as being, two meters high by one meter wide. He could not see the lower portion of the figure as it was standing behind a brick dividing wall separating the neighbor's yard and was about 80cm high.

The witness could not distinguish the figure's arms or legs. As soon as he noticed the strange being the witness was possessed with a state of panic, he remained staring at the figure for a brief time, glued to the ground and unable to move, perhaps due to the state of shock in which he found himself. As soon as he managed to move, which did not take long, he ran out of the yard into his house and ran upstairs where he came across his mother and wife who became quite alarmed as to his condition. His hair was standing up on end and had turned whiter than "whitewash". For more than three days after the incident the witness refused to speak about the incident, and even today reticently does so. Due to the fact that the witness fled the area he did not see the strange luminous figure depart.
