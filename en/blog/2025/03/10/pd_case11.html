THE ET IN THE RAIN. One night in October 1995, two 13-year-old girls were horseback riding through the woods near their homes in Breitbrunn, Germany. Without warning, they came upon a dome-shaped silver saucer covered with colored lights sitting on the ground, and next to it were little “munchkin-sized” humanoids.
